Remembering Dad

10 November 2026

I recently lost my dad, and I wanted to honour him by sharing a few stories about the man who helped shape my life.

Alongside the sadness, there are memories that still make me smile.

When I was about four years old, I proudly told my friends:

“My father is better than yours because he knows so many policemen. He can easily put you in jail!”

To four-year-old me, that made him a very powerful man. I really looked up to him.

Then there was one Christmas with Dad. He loved chapati, so he decided to make some.

The wheat flour ran out. It was late, so he used maize flour to finish them.

They looked like chapati, but tasted like ugali!

I have never forgotten those chapatis.

One thing I really admired about Dad was how much he valued education. He believed it was an equaliser, something that could open doors and change your life.

He encouraged his siblings, his children and others around him to get education. He worked hard to help make that possible.

I remember him sitting us down, giving us advice and talking about our future.

He cared about how we were doing and wanted to see us succeed. He showed that same concern for the other people in his life.

I will miss sitting down and talking with you.

Rest in peace, Dad. We love you.
