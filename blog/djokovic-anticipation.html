What Djokovic Taught Me About Anticipation

2026

I just watched Djokovic return 100 times.

He hit a 200 km/h serve back in 400 milliseconds.

I tried the same return in my head and I was still thinking 3 seconds later.

Here is the difference. He reads the serve before it is hit. Server’s shoulder = direction. Ball toss = spin. Racquet angle = speed. All of it = shot already chosen before contact. I would wait to see the ball, then react.

His brain works ahead of time. Mine works in real time.

He moves with the ground. Split-step = legs loaded like a spring. Foot stamps down = ground pushes back. Hips uncoil = shoulders follow. Racquet arm last = 150 km/h at contact. I would swing with my arm.

The ground does half the work for him. I never even notice it is there.

He resets before the ball lands. Ball leaves strings = feet already moving. Moving back to center = ready position. Next point begins = before this one ends. Result = always one shot ahead. I would watch my shot and hope.

He is already in the next point. I am still in the last one.

The lesson is not “swing harder.” Most of the work happens before the ball is even hit.
